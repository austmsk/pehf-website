## Design Context

### Users

**Primary audience:** People discovering or evaluating PEHF for the first time — potential donors, grant funders, community members seeking services, and volunteers. The majority are in early "understanding" mode: they want to know who PEHF is, what they do, and whether they can trust them before taking any action.

**Secondary audiences:** Returning donors reviewing impact, press/media, and partner organizations doing due diligence.

**Context of use:** Desktop and mobile, often during intentional research sessions. Not a utility app — users are reading and assessing, not rushing. Older adults are an explicit accommodation target: clear type hierarchy, generous spacing, no cognitive overload.

**Cultural grounding:** The foundation serves Ugandans and people of African background. Design should feel culturally resonant — warm, grounded, and human — not generic "international charity." Imagery direction: represent real African communities with dignity; avoid stock-photo tokenism.

---

### Brand Personality

Three words: **community-rooted, supportive, progressive**

- **Community-rooted**: The work comes from within affected communities, not imposed from outside. The voice is warm and personal, not distant institutional.
- **Supportive**: Encourages, uplifts, meets people where they are. Never condescending or crisis-exploiting.
- **Progressive**: Forward-looking. Not trapped in charity tropes. Shows possibility and agency, not just need.

**Emotional goal:** Visitors should feel *moved but not manipulated* — inspired by human possibility, trusting that their engagement will matter.

---

### Aesthetic Direction

**Theme:** Light. Cream backgrounds with forest green and gold accents — already defined in the design tokens. This palette has African soil warmth without being literal or clichéd.

**Visual tone:** Editorial restraint with human warmth. Not cold corporate non-profit, not generic "save Africa" charity aesthetic. Think: the confidence of a well-funded foundation combined with the intimacy of community storytelling.

**Positive references:**
- **Mastercard Foundation** (mastercardfdn.org): Strong video hero, clear editorial layout, professional without being cold. The use of large photography and clear type hierarchy is the right register.
- **IKEA Foundation** (ikeafoundation.org): Simple, breathable layouts, minimalism that lets content lead. Coloring favors restraint — nothing shouts.

**Anti-references:**
- Cold hospital/medical brochure aesthetic — clinical, sterile, distant
- Generic "poverty porn" charity aesthetics — crisis imagery, guilt-driven copy
- Slick Silicon Valley tech-nonprofit look — too polished, feels performative

**Key visual decisions:**
- Video heroes are confirmed and good (already implemented on homepage)
- Layouts should breathe — generous white space, content-led
- Photography/avatar placeholders use initials on colored divs (already established) — warm tones, not blue-grey
- Large, confident typography at section entrances; body text stays readable and generous

---

### Design Principles

1. **Dignity first.** Every design choice — imagery direction, copy tone, layout — should represent communities as full human beings with agency, not as problems to be solved.
2. **Clarity for all ages.** Older adults are an explicit audience. Type must be legible at a glance: minimum 16px body, generous line-height, clear contrast, no reliance on color alone to convey meaning.
3. **Let stories lead.** Stats matter but human narratives come first. Layout hierarchy should surface stories before numbers whenever possible.
4. **Restraint over decoration.** Every visual element earns its place. Prefer space and typography over ornamental complexity.
5. **Warmth without sentimentality.** The design should feel human and close — not manipulative or tearjerking. Confidence and care, not urgency and guilt.

---

### Technical Constraints

- Framework: Next.js 16 App Router, TypeScript, CSS Modules
- Design tokens: Locked in `app/globals.css` — use only the defined CSS variables
- Fonts: Playfair Display (display/headings) + DM Sans (body/UI) — locked by CLAUDE.md
- No Tailwind utility classes in practice; scoped CSS only
- No external UI libraries without explicit approval
- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum; older adult accommodations (generous sizing, contrast)
